# Taiwan child-rearing and household cost research for「你有資格生下一代嗎？」

Compiled 2026-10-10. Every number gives its data year and a source URL. All amounts are NT$ unless marked otherwise.

## Ground rules and caveats

- **Search budget.** The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Some items could not get a dedicated search: utilities, commercial insurance, used-car prices, 2024–26 孝親費 surveys, junior-high cram prices, and German tuition. These are marked **ESTIMATE** (reasoning given) or **UNVERIFIED** (my background knowledge, not re-checked this session).
- **Blocked or unreadable official files.** Several official files could not be read: the DGBAS full report PDF (robots), stat.gov.tw (proxy 403), and the Taipei City Health Department and Social Welfare PDFs (fetch errors). Official figures therefore come mostly through city DGBAS bulletins (台中, 桃園), news reports and secondary pages that quote them.
- **Tiers.** For each item:
  - **LOW** is a frugal but realistic family: public or quasi-public options, hand-me-downs, breastfeeding, no cram school.
  - **TYPICAL** is a median urban dual-income family.
  - **HIGH** is upper-middle class: private or bilingual school, premium products. It does not mean ultra-rich, except where a row is marked "elite".
- **Housing is excluded.** It is covered in `housing.md`.
- **Correction to the brief: foreign helpers for childcare are now allowed.** Since **2026-04-13**, any household with **one child under 12** can apply for a 外籍家庭幫傭 (foreign domestic helper). See §3.4. The game should probably include this option.

- **Flu vaccine** is publicly funded from 6 months to school entry. Self-pay costs 850–1,900. Source: [今周刊 2025-11-03](https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/183029/post/202510310045/).

### 3.3 Grandparents (祖父母帶)

- **Survey average.** In 衛福部's 111 survey (2022), families using relatives other than the parents paid **11,158 per month on average**; **54.7% pay** anything at all. Source: MOHW 兒童篇 PDF (§2).
- Same survey, average paid to a licensed childminder: 18,799. To a daycare center: 14,693. Kindergarten: 7,334. Average childcare fee for ages 0–3 overall: 11,728. These are amounts actually paid in 2022, mostly after subsidy.
- **Anecdote:** one family paid grandparents 20,000 per month for the first child and 25,000 for the second (2024). Source: vocus 琲珈 (§2).

**Tiers:** LOW 0 (unpaid, about 45% of families); TYPICAL ~11,000; HIGH ~25,000.

### 3.4 Correction: foreign domestic helpers are now allowed for families with children

- **New rule from 2026-04-13.** A household with **at least one child under 12** can apply. About **1.44 million** households qualify (Ministry of Labor). Sources: [CNA 2026-03-18](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/aipl/202603180239.aspx); [風傳媒 2026-04-14](https://www.storm.mg/lifestyle/11120991).
- **Monthly cost to the employer, standard household:**
  - Salary 20,000
  - 健保 1,428 (nownews uses 1,286)
  - 職災 insurance 56
  - 就業安定費 **5,000**; 2,000 for single-parent, disability or rare-disease households
  - Sunday overtime ~2,668 (4 days at 667)
  - Agency service fee ~1,500–2,000

  The listed items sum to roughly **26,300–31,000**. Storm quotes **29,000–34,000**. A [nownews 2026-03-19](https://www.nownews.com/news/6797633) baseline is 26,334 for a standard household and 23,334 for a special household. Another source gives an average of 29,314 for Taiwanese employers ([asiapacific](https://asiapacific.tw/foreign-domestic-helper/)). Food and lodging are extra.
- **Scope limit.** Helpers may not do nursing or medical care. Fines for out-of-scope work are up to 150,000 per Storm's article body; its headline says 15,000, which conflicts.

**Tiers for the game:** LOW 26,000 (special household, no Sunday work); TYPICAL 31,000; HIGH 35,000 (including food and lodging).

## 14. Retirement (brief)

- **勞保 old-age annuity:**
  - Average **19,312 per month** at end-2025 across 2,015,310 recipients. Men 20,632; women 18,212.
  - **53.55% receive less than 20,000.**
  - Average 30.08 insured years; average insured salary 38,119.
  - Source: [udn 2026-02-25](https://udn.com/news/story/7269/9343269).
- **Statutory 勞保 formula (UNVERIFIED this session):** average insured salary × years × 1.55%. The maximum insured salary is 45,800.
  - Median-ish earner at the 45,800 cap for 30 years ≈ **21,300 per month**.
  - Someone at ~38,000 for 30 years ≈ 17,700.
- **勞退 (6% employer-funded account): ESTIMATE ~8,000–9,500 per month.**
  - Reasoning: 6% × 45,800 = 2,748 per month for 30 years at 3–4% returns gives a balance of ≈157–185萬.
  - Drawn down over 20 years at about 2%, that pays ≈7,900–9,400 per month.
  - Voluntary 6% employee contributions roughly double it.
- **Commonly cited need:** at least **40,000 per month** in retirement, implying a gap of **20,000+ per month** for an average 勞保-only retiree ([旺得富 2026-02-27](https://wantrich.chinatimes.com/news/20260227900005-420501)).
- With the 勞退 estimate included, the gap is ≈ **11,000–13,000 per month** (ESTIMATE).
- The 勞保 fund ran a deficit for the 9th straight year in 2025 (70.7 billion); a 101.5 billion shortfall is projected for 2026.

## Savings assumption (surplus saved)

- Each simulated year, after-tax income plus transfers minus all modelled spending is the surplus. By default the whole surplus is saved (`adv.saveRate = 1`).
- This is optimistic. Weddings, gadgets, emergencies and student-loan repayment are not modelled.
- Benchmarks:
  - DGBAS 2024 Family Income and Expenditure Survey: the average household saves about 23.7% of disposable income (27.7萬/yr; storm.mg summary).
  - yes123 2026 survey: workers aged 39 or under self-report average savings of only 14.9萬; 22.4% have none and 40.2% run a monthly deficit (money.udn.com). This is a self-selected online sample and probably excludes investments.
- Players can set `adv.saveRate` to 0.8 or 0.6 under 進階假設. The unsaved part of a positive surplus is booked as `exp.extra`; deficit years are unaffected.
- `exp.extra` is excluded from `childCost`, so the per-child cost does not depend on this setting.
- Sensitivity, from the same base case: dual earners aged 25 with zero savings, first child at 31, 2 kids, used flat bought the year before the first birth. Saving 2萬/month less for the 5 years before buying raises the threshold PR as follows:

  | Taichung, buy | +10 |
  | Kaohsiung, buy | +12 |
  | Taipei, rent | +12.6 |
  | Kaohsiung, rent | +14 |
