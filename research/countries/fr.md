## 摘要（繁體中文）

法國常被當作「對家庭友善」的對照組。整體來看，一對收入在中位數附近的法國雙薪夫妻，稅後年收入約 5.0–5.2 萬歐元（約 180–185 萬台幣）。生了兩個孩子之後，他們的所得稅幾乎歸零：家庭商數制讓每個孩子多半個份額。他們還可以領到以下支援，多數有所得門檻：生育一次給付、3 歲前每月 PAJE 基本津貼、第 2 胎起的家庭津貼。托育費用依家庭所得比例計算，3 歲起公立幼兒學校免費，大學註冊費一年 178 歐元。

另一面是房價。巴黎老公寓每平方公尺 9,560 歐元，換算約 **113 萬台幣／坪**，已經和台北市的 107 萬／坪相當，而且法國算的是室內淨面積，台灣權狀坪含公設。巴黎近郊、里昂、中型城市則便宜很多。法國的生育率 2025 年也降到 1.56，是戰後首次出現死亡數多於出生數。可見福利好也擋不住生育率下滑。

### Structural differences the engine would need

1. **Joint taxation with the quotient familial.** Tax = barème(R / parts) × parts. Each child adds 0.5 part (1 from the 3rd child). The advantage per half-part is capped at 1,807 €. Then a décote applies to low tax amounts (couple: 1,483 € − 45.25% × tax, if tax < 3,277 €). Taiwan's model uses per-person exemptions and deductions. France needs a household-level function.
2. **Benefits are tested on income from 2 years earlier (N-2).** For 2026, the test uses 2024 "revenu net catégoriel". The threshold also differs for "couple with one income" vs "couple with two incomes" (each partner earns ≥ 6,306 €). The engine must keep lagged income and separate thresholds per benefit.
3. **Child benefits start at the 2nd child**, are paid until age 20, and are reduced (halved or quartered) above income thresholds. This adds an explicit 2nd-child incentive.
4. **Childcare cost is a share of income, not a flat fee minus subsidy.** Crèche (PSU): hourly fee = monthly resources × rate (0.0619% for 1 child, 0.0516% for 2, 0.0413% for 3). Resources are floored at 814.62 € and capped at 8,500 €/month. Assistante maternelle (CMG since Sept 2025): CMG = cost × (1 − resources × rate / 4.91) [DERIVED from the published examples]. Then a 50% tax credit on up to 3,500 €/child/yr of out-of-home care for children under 6.
5. **Free, compulsory school from age 3.** Taiwan's 2–6 kindergarten stage collapses to 0–3 childcare plus canteen and after-school costs.
6. **Social contributions are proportional.** There are no insured-salary bands like 勞保 or 健保. Part of the CSG (2.4%) and the CRDS (0.5%) are not deductible, so they are added back to taxable income.
7. **Credit is limited by law.** Debt service must stay ≤ 35% of income, including insurance. The maximum term is 25 years (27 for off-plan purchases). There is no 寬限期 grace period except the PTZ deferral.
8. **Floor area definitions differ.** Use m² habitable in France vs 權狀坪 in Taiwan. If the game shows "price per 坪", French prices should be shown per net 坪 with a note, or converted with an assumed common-area ratio.
9. **Leave design.** Maternity leave is 16 weeks at about net salary, capped at 104.02 €/day. Paternity leave is 25 days plus 3 birth-leave days. The new congé de naissance gives 1–2 months per parent. After that, PreParE pays a low flat 459.70 €/month for up to 24 months per parent (from the 2nd child).
10. **Rent regime.** Paris and Lyon have rent control (encadrement), and social housing is a large, income-capped option (ceiling 81,858 € for 4 persons in Paris). A "social housing" choice would be a meaningful option, but waiting times were **not found**.

## Ground rules and caveats

- **Access limits.** Direct `curl` to every host (insee.fr, worldbank.org, bot.com.tw) was blocked by the egress proxy. All data came through the fetch tool, which cannot read XLSX/CSV downloads or chart images. INSEE tables available only as files (Budget de famille TF105/TF106, salary distribution XLSX) were therefore not read.
- **Search budget.** The shared web-search budget ran out near the end of the session. The following were **not** searched: APL amounts, social-housing share, notary fees, canteen prices, tutoring prices, Paris rents by size, the 2025–26 wage growth rate, the Taiwan PPP factor, and the mean age at first birth. These are marked "not found" or UNVERIFIED.
- **Labels.**
  - **[OFFICIAL]** read on an INSEE, CAF, service-public, info.gouv, Notaires, HCFEA, DREES or Cnaf page or PDF.
  - **[NEWS]** media or aggregator quoting an official source.
  - **[PORTAL]** private listing or index data (SeLoger, MeilleursAgents).
  - **[DERIVED]** my own arithmetic on sourced numbers. The method is shown.
  - **[UNVERIFIED]** background knowledge, not checked this session.
- **"Gross" (brut) vs "net".** French payslips quote gross. INSEE headline medians are net (after employee contributions, before income tax). Taiwan's DGBAS 總薪資 is pre-contribution, so French **gross** is the closer comparison.

For modelling annual cash flow, annual tax = the barème result. Withholding timing is a cash-flow detail only [UNVERIFIED detail].

## 14. Round 2 additions (2026-10-12)

### 14.1 City consumption (Paris)

INSEE, Budget de famille 2017 ("Les comportements de consommation en 2017", https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/4127596) [OFFICIAL]:
- France average spending per household is 33,970 € a year, or 22,220 € per consumption unit (UC).
- In the Paris agglomeration, spending per UC is **+18%** above the national average, at 39,410 € per household.
- In urban units of 100,000–1,999,999 people (the band Lyon is in), spending per UC is **0%** above average.
- Imputed rents are excluded.

Paris city proper has higher incomes than its agglomeration, and its owners' imputed rents are large, so the model uses `cons` **1.2** for Paris. This is a conservative value. Lyon stays at 1.0.

### 14.3 Prime d'activité (from 1 April 2026), now modeled

- Montant forfaitaire 638.28 € (décret n° 2026-222). Earned income counts at **59.85%**. The bonification individuelle starts at a net income of **709.18 €** a month. Forfait logement is 76.59 / 153.19 / 189.57 € [OFFICIAL] https://www.economie.gouv.fr/node/34212.
- Base by household [SECONDARY] https://www.previssima.fr/actualite/prime-dactivite-quels-sont-les-nouveaux-montants-au-1er-avril-2026.html:
  - single person 638.28 €
  - couple, or single parent with one child: 957.42 €
  - couple with one child: 1,148.90 €
  - couple with two children: 1,340.38 €
  - each further dependant: +255.31 €
- 2026 reform: the bonification maximum rises from about 184–186 € to about 239–241 €, reached at 1.15 SMIC (about 1,699 € net social) instead of 1 SMIC [NEWS] https://www.journaldeleconomie.fr/prime-d-activite-reforme-2026/.
- Model formula: forfaitaire + 59.85% × net earnings + bonifications − max(forfaitaire, net earnings + family benefits (PAJE base, AF, CF, PreParE) + forfait logement).
  - Forfait logement is counted for owners, for people housed by family, and for APL recipients.
  - The model uses yearly averages, and the benefit is paid only when it is at least 15 € a month.
- Effect at default settings: Lyon's threshold PR changes 42.1 → 41.5.
