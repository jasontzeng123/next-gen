## 摘要（繁體中文）

南韓與台灣的結構很像：生育率極低、首都房價高、補習普遍。差別在於南韓**頭兩年的現金補助比台灣多很多**，但**首爾房價和貸款管制更嚴**。另外，南韓租屋最常見的形式是「全稅（전세）」：一次付一大筆押金，之後不付月租。

### 三個最關鍵的結構差異

1. **全稅（전세）**：租屋者一次付約房價 50–60% 的押金，期滿全額退還，期間不付月租。成本是押金貸款的利息，加上自有資金的機會成本。以首爾平均押金 6.37 億推估，每月約 219 萬韓元，與月租型（約 216 萬）幾乎相同（§4.4，推估）。
2. **個人分開報稅**：南韓沒有夫妻合併申報，台灣模型的合併申報邏輯要改。健保也不因眷屬多寡加收保費。
3. **買房貸款管制極嚴**：首爾全區與京畿 12 地區是管制區，一般 LTV 只有 40%。房價 15 億以下最多貸 6 億，15–25 億最多 4 億，25 億以上最多 2 億。以首爾中位數房價計算，自備款約 7.75 億韓元（≈1,845 萬台幣）。

### Structural differences the model would need

1. **Jeonse tenure.** Add a third housing mode alongside buy and rent. The tenant pays a deposit D, about 50–60% of the price (Seoul 50.9% in Jan 2026), usually for a 2-year term. D is financed by a jeonse loan (bank average 4.35%, Aug 2026; policy 버팀목 / 신생아 특례 1.3–4.3%) plus own savings. The yearly cost is loan interest plus the forgone return on own funds. **D is returned in full at the end**, so it counts as an asset. Model renewal as D growing with jeonse prices; jeonse prices rose 14.9% in Seoul over Aug 2024 – Aug 2026. Monthly rent with a deposit (보증부 월세) is now the majority form in Seoul. Optionally add a deposit-loss risk (전세사기); no frequency data was gathered.
2. **Individual taxation.** There is no married joint filing, and the child credit only starts at age 8.
3. **Front-loaded cash benefits.** In the first 2 years, a 1st child brings 200만 (voucher) + 1,200만 + 600만 (부모급여) + 240만 (아동수당) = **2,240만 KRW ≈ 53萬 TWD** [DERIVED], before local grants. Taiwan's model gives about 10萬 + 12–14萬 TWD over the same period.
4. **Loan caps.** Use absolute caps by price band plus DSR (bank limit 40%, UNVERIFIED this session 〔now confirmed [SECONDARY], §12.3.5〕), not just LTV.
5. **Universal free childcare** via vouchers. Out-of-pocket costs are only extras, which removes most of Taiwan's `CARE` branch.
6. **Regions.** Use about 5 regions instead of Taiwan's 22 counties: Seoul (강남 11 / 강북 14 districts as the core/outer split), Gyeonggi, Incheon, Busan, and other.
7. **Military service.** Men serve about 18 months (UNVERIFIED this session), which shifts the age–wage curve.
8. **Postpartum centres** are standard (2 weeks) rather than optional.

## 0. Ground rules and data access

- Labels follow the Taiwan files:
  - **[OFFICIAL]** read on a government page or an official dataset.
  - **[NEWS]** a news report quoting an official release.
  - **[SECONDARY]** a non-government summary site.
  - **[DERIVED]** my own arithmetic; the method is shown.
  - **[ESTIMATE]** my judgement.
  - **[UNVERIFIED]** background knowledge not re-checked this session.
- **Blocked sites.** These official sites refused the fetch tool (robots): `bokjiro.go.kr`, `korea.kr`, `nts.go.kr`, `index.go.kr`, `api.db.nomics.world`. **How jeonse works [DERIVED example, Seoul apartment average]**

- The tenant deposits D = 6.37억 for 2 years. The landlord returns D at the end.
- Assume 80% of D comes from a jeonse loan at the bank average of 4.35% (Aug 2026, [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000337490)), and 20% from own savings forgoing the 3.21% deposit rate.
- Yearly cost = 6.37억 × (0.8 × 4.35% + 0.2 × 3.21%) ≈ **2,624만/yr ≈ 219만/month**.
- The equivalent 월세 package costs 164.1만 + 1.96억 × 3.21% / 12 ≈ **216만/month**. At the 4.7% Seoul conversion rate, D equals about 249만/month.
- So the three ways of costing it come out about the same. Jeonse's main cost to a young couple is the up-front lump sum, which is about 6.1 years of median pay [DERIVED: 6.37억 / 3,456만]. A KB "J-PIR" for Seoul of 5.1–6.6 years by income quintile ([Herald](https://heraldk.com/2026/05/29/%ec%84%9c%ec%9a%b8-%ec%a0%80%ec%86%8c%eb%93%9d%ec%b8%b5-%ed%95%9c-%ed%91%bc%eb%8f%84-%ec%95%88-%ec%93%b0%ea%b3%a0-112%eb%85%84-%eb%aa%a8%ec%95%84%ec%95%bc-%ea%b0%95%eb%82%a8-%ec%a7%91-%ec%82%b0/)) confirms the scale.
- UNVERIFIED: the standard contract is 2 years, with one tenant renewal right ("2+2") and a 5% cap on increases at renewal. 〔Superseded: confirmed [SECONDARY], see §12.3.4〕

#### 12.3.4 Lease rules (verified)

- Lease term **2 years**. The tenant may use the **renewal right once**, for **2 more years** ("2+2").
- Rent or deposit can rise **at most 5%** at renewal. No further rise is allowed within one year of an increase. City or provincial ordinances can set a lower cap.
- The landlord may refuse renewal on 9 grounds, including moving in themselves. If the landlord then re-lets within the 2 years, damages are owed.

### 13.2 Jeonse deposit, now modeled as cash

- Jeonse loans cover up to 80% of the deposit. The HF general jeonse guarantee caps them at 4억 [SECONDARY] https://wikidocs.net/blog/@Insight_Lab/24298/ (2026-07).
- **Own cash** is the rest of the deposit. Its cost is 2% inflation a year, because the deposit earns no interest, plus the savings return the household gives up.
- A household puts at most half of its savings plus existing deposit into the deposit.
- If that is short, the deposit falls and the gap is paid as monthly rent at the conversion rate: Seoul 4.7%, Busan 5.3% (national) (§4.4).
- The deposit counts as the family's money: it is returned when they buy, and it is not counted as spending.
