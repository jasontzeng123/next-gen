# Sensitivity of threshold PR to estimated inputs (2026-10-12)

Default settings:
- couple, both aged 28
- first child at 31, 2 children
- the Taipei standard choices
- renting or buying a used home

Each input group is changed on its own: costs are raised by 10%, and the wage median is lowered by 10%. The table shows how much the threshold PR moves.

**Takeaway:** the wage median matters most. A 10% error in it moves the PR about as much as a 10% error in every cost group combined.

How solid each country's median is:
- France (INSEE) and Japan (NTA brackets): solid.
- Korea (Statistics Korea jobs data, low end adjusted): good.
- China: no official median. This is the largest single uncertainty in the world comparison; see cn.md §14.5.

Among costs, adult living costs and rent come next. A city's price level and home prices matter mainly when buying.
